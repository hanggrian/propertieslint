    Key grouping#

    Keys sharing the same prefix before the first . or - must be grouped together and separated from other groups with a blank line. Keys without a separator are not grouped.

    Before ❌

    one.one=value
two.one=value
one.two=value

two.two=value

    After ✅

    one.one=value
one.two=value

two.one=value
two.two=value
